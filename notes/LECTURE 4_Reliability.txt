# Design Techniques for Reliability
# Redundancy: redundancy refers to the duplication or replication of critical components or functions within a system to enhance reliability and ensure continued operation even if one part fails
  Derating: It is the practice of operating electrical and electronic devices at less than their maximum rated capacity to improve reliability and lifespan
  Environmental protection
  Stress-strength analysis: is a reliability engineering method used to assess the probability of failure in a component or system by comparing the applied stress to its inherent strength
  Modular design: Modular design in software engineering is a design principle that involves subdividing a software system into smaller, independent, and interchangeable components called modules. Each module is designed to perform a specific function or set of related functions, and these modules collectively form the complete software system.
  Robust design (Taguchi methods): is an approach focused on creating products or processes that perform consistently well, even when faced with variations in manufacturing, usage, or environmental conditions

# Difference between quality and reliability
# The difference between quality and reliability lies in how a product performs initially versus how it continues to perform over time.
# [table] Aspect | Quality | Reliability
# [table] Timeframe | At the time of purchase/use | Over a period of time
# [table] Measure | Meets specifications and expectations | Performs consistently without failure
# [table] Example | A sharp, new LED TV with vibrant colors | A washing machine that works well for 10 years
# [table] Focus | Initial features and performance | Long-term dependability

--- Slide 26 ---
# Example: Reliable Web Server System
  Imagine you're part of a team that has developed a web server for an e-commerce website.
  ✅ High Reliability means:
  The server runs 24/7 without crashing.
  It can handle high user traffic during sales events.
  It recovers quickly from minor failures (using redundancy or failover).
  It operates for months or years without unexpected downtime.
  ❌ Low Reliability would be:
  The server crashes frequently, requiring restarts.
  It lags or becomes unresponsive under moderate load.
  Users lose data during transactions.
  It needs frequent maintenance or patching to function properly.

# Case Study – Smartphone Battery Reliability
# Frequent customer complaints on overheating
  Root cause: Charging circuit component failure
  Solution: Improved thermal pad placement + component derating
  Outcome: MTBF improved by 45%
