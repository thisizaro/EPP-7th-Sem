Aranya is taking **EX40003 Engineering Professional Practice** (KIIT, School of Mechanical
Engineering, Spring Semester 2026, L-T-P 2:0:0). Course coordinator: Mr. Pradeep Kandula.
Textbook: Shrestha & Shrestha, *Textbook of Engineering Professional Practice*, 3rd ed.

**Marks:** Mid-Sem 20 (90 min, closed book) - End-Sem 50 (3 hr, closed book) -
Active learning 30 (interactive focus 12, assignment 12, quiz 6).

**Four syllabus modules, and how the slide decks in this repo map onto them:**
- Module I - Engineering and Engineer -> LECTURE 1, LECTURE 2
- Module II - Selected Functions of Engineering -> LECTURE 3 (safety), 4 (reliability),
  5 (quality), 6 (productivity), 7 (uncertainty/risk), 8 (project mgmt), 9 (quality engineering)
- Module III - Professional Aspects of Engineering (ethics, law, IPR, WTO) -> **no slides in repo**
- Module IV - Group Dynamics (teams, stakeholders, negotiation) -> **no slides in repo**

Because Quiz-1 and the Mid-Semester Exam fall right after Module II in the lesson plan,
the decks present cover exactly the **mid-semester** scope. Two syllabus topics inside
Module II have **no deck at all**: *Managerial functions (planning, organizing, motivating,
controlling)* and *Costing and accounting* (2 lectures) - these are still examinable.

See [[epp-slide-content-gaps]] for what the decks themselves are missing.
